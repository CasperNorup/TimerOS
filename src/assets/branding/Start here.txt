TimerOS Logo Kit

APP AND PROFILE
TimerOS-app-and-profile-square.png is the opaque 1024 × 1024 profile image.
TimerOS-app-icon-rounded.png is the cobalt-ribbon T on a navy tile with transparent corners.
TimerOS-app-icon-transparent.png centers the standalone blue T on a transparent square sized to match the BudgetOS app icon.
Use it in the app header and use src-tauri/icons/timeros-transparent.ico for Windows desktop packaging.

STANDALONE MARK
TimerOS-mark-transparent.png isolates the folded-ribbon T for other layouts.

WEBSITE HEADERS
Use the light-background wordmark on light surfaces and the dark-background wordmark on dark surfaces.
Both PNGs have transparent backgrounds.

BROWSER TABS AND PRINT
favicon.ico includes 16, 32, and 48 px icons. TimerOS-favicon-32.png is the 32 px PNG.
The black and white print SVGs use the ribbon silhouette and outlined lettering.

PORTRAIT SPLASH
TimerOS-splash-screen.png is the static 852 × 1846 launch screen.
TimerOS-splash-screen-animated.mp4 and TimerOS-splash-screen-lottie.json contain the 3.2-second Classic reveal.
Bloom, Origami, and Signal each run four seconds in MP4 and Lottie JSON.

ROUNDED ICON MOTION
Bloom opens the ribbon with blue glints. Origami brings the crossbar and stem together. Signal scans the mark into view.
All animations run at 30 fps. MP4s are silent with an opaque navy matte. Lottie files embed their own artwork.
